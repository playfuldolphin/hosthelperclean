Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

How is this different from hiring a cleaner directly?

When you hire directly, you're responsible for scheduling, quality control, payments, and finding backup if they cancel. We handle all of that. You get automated checklists, photo verification, insurance coverage, and backup cleaners—all in one platform. Plus, our cleaners are vetted and rated by other hosts.

What if my cleaner doesn't show up?

We have backup cleaners in every market. If your assigned cleaner can't make it, we automatically notify backup cleaners in your area. In 99% of cases, we find coverage within 2 hours. If we can't, you don't pay.

How do payments work?

We charge your card after each cleaning is completed and verified. You'll see the full price (including our fee) before booking. No hidden charges, no monthly subscriptions. We pay cleaners directly, so you don't have to worry about invoices or tip calculations.

Are your cleaners insured and background checked?

Yes! All cleaners go through background checks and are covered by our $1M liability insurance policy. They also maintain a minimum 4.5-star rating. If a cleaner's rating drops, they're removed from the platform.

Can I use my own cleaner on your platform?

Absolutely! You can invite your existing cleaner to join the platform. They'll get access to your checklists, photo verification tools, and automated payment features. It's free for them to join, and you both benefit from better organization.

What's your refund policy?

If you're not satisfied with a cleaning, report it within 24 hours. We'll either send the cleaner back to fix the issues (free of charge) or give you a full refund. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
