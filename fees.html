Service Fees

Host Helper Clean charges a service fee to help us maintain the platform, provide customer support, process payments securely, and ensure quality service.

How Fees Work

When you book a cleaning service, you'll see the total price which includes:

  • The cleaner's rate for the service
  • A service fee (typically around 15%)

Transparent Pricing

Before you confirm any booking, you'll see a complete price breakdown showing:

  • Base cleaning rate
  • Any add-on services
  • Service fee
  • Total amount you'll pay

What the Service Fee Covers

  • 24/7 customer support
  • Secure payment processing
  • Platform maintenance and improvements
  • Cleaner background checks and vetting
  • Quality guarantee and dispute resolution
  • Insurance coverage

High Volume Users

Users who book frequently (20+ cleanings per month) automatically receive reduced service fees. This discount is applied automatically to your account.
